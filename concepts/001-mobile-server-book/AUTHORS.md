## Development history

### Stage 1 — Initial brainstorming

The core idea was developed by the author in a brainstorming session with **Google Gemini**.
This session produced the first sketches, the general direction, and the initial feature list.

### Stage 2 — Engineering concept

The author then developed the full, detailed concept in collaboration with **DeepSeek**.
This stage produced:

- the problem statement and value proposition,
- the two-board architecture,
- the network, cooling, power, and enclosure layers,
- the bill of materials and power budget,
- the tradeoffs document and the 6-month roadmap,
- the future ideas parking lot.

All engineering decisions, scope cuts, and final wording were made by the author.
The AI assistant served as a structured brainstorming partner, critic, and technical editor.
